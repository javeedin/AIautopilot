# AIautopilot ERP System

## Overview
AIautopilot is a comprehensive ERP system inspired by Oracle Fusion ERP, designed to provide enterprise-level financial and operational management capabilities.

## Project Scope
The system will include the following modules:
- **General Ledger (GL)** - Financial accounting and reporting
- **Accounts Payable (AP)** - Vendor invoice and payment management
- **Accounts Receivable (AR)** - Customer invoice and receipt management
- **Order Management (OM)** - Sales order processing
- **Cash Management (CM)** - Cash and bank reconciliation
- **Fixed Assets (FA)** - Asset lifecycle management
